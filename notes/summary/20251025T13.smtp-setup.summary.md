## Knowledge Gained

### DKIM History Lesson
- **SMTP (1982):** No security, anyone can claim to be anyone
- **SPF (2000-2014):** Authorizes IP addresses, but breaks on forwarding
- **DKIM (2004-2007):** Cryptographic signatures, survives forwarding
  - Merger of Yahoo's DomainKeys + Cisco's IIM
  - Early adopters: Gmail, Yahoo, AOL, Fastmail
- **DMARC (2010-2015):** Policy layer on top of SPF/DKIM
- **2025 mandate:** Google/Yahoo require DKIM for >5,000 emails/day

### Google Workspace for Nonprofits
- Free tier: 2,000 users at $0/month
- Includes: Gmail, Calendar, Meet, Drive, Gemini
- Paid tiers: $3-5/user (vs $6-18 commercial)
- User accounts ARE mailboxes (no separate concept)
- Service accounts NOT recommended for simple email sending
