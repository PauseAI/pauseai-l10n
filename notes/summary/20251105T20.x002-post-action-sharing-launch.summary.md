# X002 Experiment Launch: Post-Action Sharing A/B Test

**Date:** 2025-11-05
**Session Duration:** ~45 minutes
**Branch:** main (pauseai-collagen)

## Objective

Launch X002 A/B test to measure the impact of post-action social sharing on viral growth, comparing enhanced sharing CTA vs hidden sharing controls.

## Changes Implemented

### 1. Experiment Framework
- **Defined X002_POST_ACTION_SHARING** experiment in `lib/experiments.py`
- Uses standard vowel/consonant email split (20v20 sample)
- Follows numbered experiment pattern established with X001

### 2. Email Template Updates (`lib/email_template.py`)
- **Control variant:** Adds `&x002` parameter to subscribe/validate URLs
  - Parameter triggers sharing hide on pauseai.info/join and /sayno pages
  - Maintains default sharing CTA text: "Share this with your networks:"
- **Treatment variant:** Enhanced sharing CTA
  - Text: "Tell your friends! Multiply your impact by inviting your network to sign too."
  - No x002 parameter - sharing visible on website post-action
- Import fix: Changed `from lib.experiments` to `from experiments` (sys.path already configured)

### 3. Lambda Infrastructure (`lambda/tracking_router.py`)
- Added x002 query parameter pass-through
- Appends `&x002` to subscribe and validate redirect URLs when present
- Deployed to production Lambda: `collagen-tracking-router`

### 4. Website Integration (pauseai-website PR #536)
- **SelfieUX.svelte:** Check `$page.url.searchParams.has('x002')` to conditionally hide sharing
- **join.md:** Same x002 parameter check for conditional sharing display
- **ShareButtons.svelte:** Reusable component with official platform SVG icons
  - Scaled from 1rem (footer) to 2rem (buttons)
  - Orange brand background with white icons/text
  - 5 platforms: Facebook, Twitter/X, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Reddit
- **share/+page.svelte:** Lightweight dedicated share page

### 5. Sample Selection & Deployment
- Selected 40 users (20 control, 20 treatment) from 190 not-yet-emailed sayno users
- Sample file: `scripts/X002_POST_ACTION_SHARING.txt`
- No overlap with X001 sample (80 unique users total across both experiments)
- Emails sent at 20:53-20:55 UTC on 2025-11-05
- **100% send success rate** (40/40 emails delivered)

## Results (Initial - 5 minutes post-send)

**Engagement Speed:**
- **25/39 opens within 5 minutes** (64% open rate) - likely inflated by email client pre-fetching
- **Real human actions within 3-6 minutes:**
  - Example: ai.wallace108@spamgourmet.com shared to Facebook 3.7 min after email
  - Example: ab@arjunbasu.com subscribed 14 seconds after opening email

**Early Stats (5 min post-send):**
- Control: 12 opens, 0 validates, 0 subscribes, 0 shares
- Treatment: 13 opens, 3 validates, 3 subscribes, 1 share
- **Treatment +7.7pp share rate** (1/13 vs 0/12) - too early for significance

**Note:** "Opens" may be inflated by automated email client image pre-fetching (some as fast as 4 seconds). Validate/subscribe/share events are confirmed human actions.

## Technical Decisions

1. **URL parameter approach:** Used opaque `x002` parameter instead of semantic `hide_sharing` to avoid alarming users
2. **Control vs Treatment logic in email generation:** Kept URL generation clean, handled experiment branching in email content generation
3. **Import path fix:** Changed to relative import since sys.path already includes lib/ directory
4. **4-second opens:** Identified as email client pre-fetch, not genuine engagement
5. **Allowlist removal for testing:** Temporarily removed to test override uid, then restored with X002 sample
