## What was accomplished

### 1. Matching-aware donation progress meter
- Created API endpoint `/api/donorbox-meter/[slug]/+server.ts` that:
  - Proxies DonorBox's goal meter embed
  - Applies matching logic: donations doubled up to half the goal (€10,500 of €21,000)
  - Parses European number format (dots as thousand separators, commas as decimals)
